### About the translations

This guide is authored in English. The Russian and Uzbek versions are translations of the English text, most of them produced by machine translation with AI. Editors are reviewing the translations; a page that has not been reviewed yet says so in a notice at the top.

Where a translation and the English text differ, the English version takes precedence.

### Reporting a translation problem

If a translation is wrong, unclear or missing, open an issue in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/uzinfocom-org/digital-health-ig/issues) with a link to the page, the language and the wording you suggest.
